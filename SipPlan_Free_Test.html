Privacy & safety

SipPlan stores this device’s data in browser storage. When you use cloud sync or AI, selected profile, hydration, and chat context is sent to your configured backend. Do not enter medical records or anything you would not want processed by the service providers. Pair sharing is for daily hydration progress—not diagnosis or medical monitoring.
